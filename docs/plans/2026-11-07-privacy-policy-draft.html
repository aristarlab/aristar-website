[확인 필요] 국내 기준(만 14세)과 COPPA 기준(13세)을 함께 다루는 이중 기준 작성 방식의 적절성은 1차 자료로 확인되지 않았으며 변호사 확인이 필요합니다.

7. Children's Privacy (Important)

esay memory eng is intended for users of all ages and will be registered on Google Play as a service that includes children.

  • We do not serve interest-based advertising or remarketing ads to any user, including children.
  • We do not transmit advertising ID, SIM Serial, Build Serial, BSSID, MAC address, SSID, IMEI, or IMSI from children or users whose age cannot be verified; we use App Set ID as the alternative for analytics purposes.

[Review opinion] The statements above describe the level required by the Google Play Families Policy. Whether the actual AdMob/Firebase SDK configuration (child-directed treatment, non-personalized ads only, etc.) is actually set up to match this description must be confirmed by the engineering team. A mismatch would create a policy-violation risk.

Korea's Personal Information Protection Act requires the consent of a legal guardian to process the personal information of children under 14 (Article 22-2). The U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) separately applies to children under 13. Because this app targets all ages, we operate with both standards in mind.

[Needs verification] Whether Article 22-2 of the Personal Information Protection Act applies even to an app like this one, which stores learning records only on the device and never transmits them to a server, requires attorney review.

[Needs verification] The appropriateness of drafting under a dual standard combining the domestic threshold (14) and the COPPA threshold (13) has not been confirmed with primary sources and requires attorney review.

10. 광고 식별자 재설정 방법

Android 기기에서 광고 식별자(광고 ID)는 다음과 같이 재설정하거나 삭제할 수 있습니다 (Android 버전에 따라 메뉴 경로가 다를 수 있습니다).

광고 ID를 삭제하면 당사 및 광고 제공자는 해당 식별자를 이용한 맞춤형 광고를 제공할 수 없습니다.

10. How to Reset the Advertising Identifier

On an Android device, the advertising identifier (advertising ID) can be reset or deleted as follows (the menu path may differ depending on the Android version).

Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy) > Delete or reset advertising ID

Once the advertising ID is deleted, we and our advertising provider can no longer deliver personalized ads based on that identifier.
